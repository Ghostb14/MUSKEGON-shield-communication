# Officer-dispatch templates

Use plain language and provide only the information needed for a safe, lawful response. Confirm the location before transmitting an assignment. Do not broadcast unnecessary personal information.

## New assignment

```text
[UNIT], respond to [EXACT LOCATION] for [REQUEST TYPE].

Reported at [TIME] by [SOURCE]:
[ONE- OR TWO-SENTENCE FACTUAL SUMMARY]

Current conditions: [HAPPENING NOW / NO LONGER OCCURRING / UNKNOWN]
Known risks: [FACTS / NONE REPORTED / UNKNOWN]
Contact: [NAME OR ROLE AND SAFE MEETING POINT / NO CONTACT REQUESTED]
Requested action: [OBSERVE / CONTACT AUTHORIZED PERSON / PROPERTY CHECK / OTHER POLICY-AUTHORIZED ACTION]

Acknowledge and advise ETA.
```

## Urgent assignment

Use only when the facts support urgency.

```text
Urgent traffic for [UNIT]. Respond to [EXACT LOCATION].

Reported emergency: [PLAIN-LANGUAGE DESCRIPTION]
Known injuries, fire, weapons, or threats: [FACTS / UNKNOWN]
Police, fire, or EMS status: [NOTIFIED AT TIME / BEING CONTACTED / UNKNOWN]
Immediate instruction: [POLICY-AUTHORIZED INSTRUCTION]

Acknowledge immediately. Advise dispatch of any change.
```

An internal dispatch message does not replace a 911 call when emergency services are needed.

## Additional information

> [UNIT], update for [CALL ID]: [NEW OR CORRECTED FACT]. The exact location is [LOCATION]. Conditions are now [STATUS]. Acknowledge the update.

## Change of assignment

> [UNIT], hold your response to [CALL ID]. [REASON, IF APPROPRIATE]. Remain [AVAILABLE / ASSIGNED TO OTHER CALL] and acknowledge.

## Cancellation

> [UNIT], cancel response to [CALL ID] at [LOCATION]. Reason: [CALLER CANCELLED / CLIENT CANCELLED / EMERGENCY SERVICES ASSUMED RESPONSE / DUPLICATE CALL / OTHER]. Do not continue unless reassigned. Acknowledge.

## Welfare check

> [UNIT], conduct a policy-authorized welfare check at [EXACT LOCATION] for [NAME OR DESCRIPTION]. Request came from [AUTHORIZED SOURCE] at [TIME]. Concern reported: [FACTUAL REASON]. Known medical or safety information: [RELEVANT FACTS / NONE PROVIDED]. Police or EMS status: [STATUS]. Acknowledge and advise ETA.

## Unable to locate

> [UNIT], copy unable to locate at [LOCATION] as of [TIME]. Confirm the areas checked, any contact attempts, and whether follow-up information is needed before clearing the call.
