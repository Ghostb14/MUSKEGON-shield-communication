# Call-intake template

Use this form for calls, texts, or in-person requests before assigning an officer. Record what the caller reports without presenting it as a verified fact.

## Immediate screening

Ask these first:

1. What is the exact location?
2. Is anyone in immediate danger?
3. Is there a fire, serious injury, medical emergency, active violence, or a reported weapon?
4. What is happening right now?
5. What is the safest callback number?

For an emergency, follow company policy for contacting 911 and advise the caller to call 911 from a safe location. Do not tell a caller to confront, follow, or detain anyone.

## Read-back

Before ending the contact:

> To confirm, you are reporting [WHAT] at [EXACT LOCATION]. It is [HAPPENING NOW / NO LONGER OCCURRING], and [KNOWN RISK OR “NO IMMEDIATE DANGER WAS REPORTED”]. Your callback number is [NUMBER]. Is that accurate?

## Receipt message

> Your report has been received under reference [CALL ID]. If conditions change or anyone is in immediate danger, move to a safe location and call 911. Reply with any corrected location or new information. A response time is not guaranteed unless dispatch confirms one.

## Missing-location follow-up

> We need the exact property address and, if applicable, the building, floor, apartment or unit, and nearest entrance. We cannot safely send an officer to search or knock on unrelated doors without a specific location and sufficient details.
