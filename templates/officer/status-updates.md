# Officer status-update templates

Keep updates short, specific, and tied to a call ID. State what you personally observe separately from what someone tells you.

## Assigned / en route

> [UNIT] assigned to [CALL ID], en route to [LOCATION]. ETA [MINUTES OR TIME].

## Delayed

> [UNIT] delayed on [CALL ID] due to [TRAFFIC / PRIOR CALL / SAFE ACCESS ISSUE / OTHER]. Revised ETA [MINUTES OR TIME]. No change in assignment requested.

## On scene

> [UNIT] on scene at [EXACT LOCATION] at [TIME]. Initial conditions: [NORMAL / ACTIVITY OBSERVED / NO ACTIVITY VISIBLE / OTHER FACTUAL DESCRIPTION].

## Contact made

> [UNIT] made contact with [CALLER / CLIENT CONTACT / SUBJECT / OTHER ROLE] at [TIME]. Contact is [COOPERATIVE / NOT COOPERATIVE / ENDED / OTHER OBSERVABLE DESCRIPTION]. Current action: [FACTUAL, POLICY-AUTHORIZED ACTION].

## Situation update

> Update for [CALL ID]: I observed [FACTS]. [PERSON] reported [SEPARATE REPORTED INFORMATION]. Current risk is [FACTS / NONE OBSERVED / UNKNOWN]. Next action is [ACTION].

## Request routine assistance

> [UNIT] requests an additional officer at [EXACT LOCATION] for [PLAIN-LANGUAGE REASON]. Conditions are [STABLE / CHANGING / UNKNOWN]. No emergency services requested at this time.

## Emergency traffic

```text
Emergency traffic from [UNIT] at [EXACT LOCATION].

Emergency: [ACTIVE VIOLENCE / FIRE / SERIOUS INJURY / MEDICAL EMERGENCY / OTHER PLAIN-LANGUAGE FACT]
Immediate need: [POLICE / FIRE / EMS]
Description and location of danger: [BRIEF VERIFIED FACTS]
Officer status: [SAFE / INJURED / UNKNOWN]
```

Follow the emergency procedure and contact 911. Give the exact location first if the transmission may be interrupted.

## Police, fire, or EMS notified

> [UNIT/DISPATCH] contacted [POLICE / FIRE / EMS] at [TIME] for [REASON]. Agency response or incident number: [NUMBER / NOT PROVIDED]. Security personnel will [SAFE, POLICY-AUTHORIZED NEXT STEP].

## Clear

```text
[UNIT] clear of [CALL ID] at [TIME].

Disposition: [NO ISSUE FOUND / ASSISTED / WARNING OR NOTICE ISSUED / REFERRED TO CLIENT / EMERGENCY SERVICES ASSUMED / OTHER]
People contacted: [ROLE OR COUNT; OMIT UNNECESSARY PERSONAL INFORMATION]
Property or injuries: [NONE OBSERVED / FACTUAL SUMMARY]
Report: [REQUIRED / COMPLETED / PENDING / NOT REQUIRED]
Follow-up: [ACTION / NONE]
```

## Out of service

> [UNIT] out of service at [TIME] due to [MEAL / VEHICLE / EQUIPMENT / REPORT / OTHER APPROVED REASON]. Expected available time: [TIME OR UNKNOWN]. Supervisor status: [NOTIFIED / NOT REQUIRED].
