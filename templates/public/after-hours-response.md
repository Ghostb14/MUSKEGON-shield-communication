# Public and after-hours response templates

Use only language that accurately reflects current staffing and service availability. Do not promise that an officer has been dispatched unless dispatch has confirmed the assignment.

## Delayed-response auto-reply

> Thank you for contacting MUSKEGON Shield Security. All available personnel are currently assisting other calls, and your message has been received for review. If there is an immediate threat to life, active violence, fire, or a serious medical emergency, move to a safe location and call 911. For a non-emergency response, reply with the exact address, building and unit, what is happening now, a safe callback number, and any relevant person or vehicle description. A response time is not guaranteed until dispatch confirms an assignment.

## General message receipt

> Thank you for contacting MUSKEGON Shield Security. We received your message at [TIME] under reference [CALL ID]. It is currently marked [PENDING REVIEW / ASSIGNED / REFERRED]. If the situation changes or becomes an emergency, call 911 and update us when it is safe to do so.

## Details needed

> To review your request, please send:
>
> - the exact property address;
> - the building, apartment or unit, floor, and nearest entrance;
> - what is happening now;
> - when it was last observed;
> - a safe callback number; and
> - a factual person or vehicle description, if relevant.
>
> Do not approach or confront anyone to gather more information.

## Unclear apartment or unit

> We do not yet have a confirmed location. You listed [LOCATION OPTION 1] or [LOCATION OPTION 2]. Please confirm the exact building and apartment or unit. We cannot safely send an officer to wander the property or knock on unrelated doors without a specific location and enough information to identify the reported issue.

## No immediate activity

> We received your report about [ISSUE] at [LOCATION]. You indicated that it is no longer occurring. The information will be documented and reviewed for [FOLLOW-UP / FUTURE PATROL ATTENTION / CLIENT NOTIFICATION]. Call 911 if the activity returns and creates an immediate danger.

## Outside service area or authority

> Thank you for the information. This location or request is outside our current service responsibility, so we cannot dispatch an officer. For an emergency, call 911. For a non-emergency matter, contact [PROPERTY MANAGEMENT / LOCAL NON-EMERGENCY NUMBER / RESPONSIBLE SERVICE].

## Assignment confirmed

> An officer has been assigned to reference [CALL ID] at [LOCATION]. Estimated arrival is [ETA], subject to changing safety conditions and higher-priority emergencies. Stay in a safe location and do not approach the reported person or activity. Call 911 if the situation becomes an emergency.
