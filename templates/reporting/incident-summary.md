# Incident-summary template

Use this as a handoff or drafting aid for the formal incident report. Complete every required company or client form separately. Preserve original notes and evidence according to policy.

## Incident header

```text
INCIDENT ID: [ID]
CALL ID: [ID OR NONE]
DATE: [YYYY-MM-DD]
START TIME: [24-HOUR TIME]
END TIME: [24-HOUR TIME]
PROPERTY: [CLIENT OR SITE]
EXACT LOCATION: [ADDRESS / BUILDING / UNIT / SPECIFIC AREA]
INCIDENT TYPE: [TYPE]
REPORTING OFFICER: [NAME / ID]
OTHER UNITS: [NAME / ID / NONE]
SUPERVISOR: [NAME / ID / NOTIFIED AT TIME / NOT REQUIRED]
```

## Involved people

List each person once. Use roles such as caller, reporting party, witness, subject, victim, client contact, or responding agency member. Collect and disclose personal information only when authorized and necessary.

```text
ROLE: [ROLE]
NAME: [FULL NAME / REFUSED / UNKNOWN]
CONTACT: [AUTHORIZED CONTACT INFORMATION / NOT COLLECTED]
IDENTIFYING DESCRIPTION: [RELEVANT FACTS]
STATEMENT OR ACTION: [FACTUAL SUMMARY]
```

## Narrative framework

```text
ASSIGNMENT
At approximately [TIME] on [DATE], I was [DISPATCHED / CONTACTED / CONDUCTING PATROL] regarding [REPORTED ISSUE] at [EXACT LOCATION]. Dispatch advised [REPORTED FACTS AND KNOWN RISKS].

ARRIVAL
I arrived at approximately [TIME]. Upon arrival, I observed [SIGHTS, SOUNDS, CONDITIONS, AND LOCATIONS]. I did not observe [ONLY MATERIAL ABSENCES, IF RELEVANT].

CONTACTS AND STATEMENTS
I contacted [PERSON AND ROLE] at [LOCATION]. [PERSON] stated, in summary, [REPORTED INFORMATION]. This information was [CONSISTENT / INCONSISTENT] with my observations in the following factual way: [FACTS]. If no contact was made, document the attempts and result.

ACTIONS
I [LIST ACTIONS IN CHRONOLOGICAL ORDER]. At [TIME], [POLICE / FIRE / EMS / SUPERVISOR / CLIENT] was notified because [FACTUAL REASON]. Instructions received or actions taken by that party were [FACTS].

DISPOSITION
At approximately [TIME], the incident was [RESOLVED / REFERRED / TRANSFERRED TO EMERGENCY SERVICES / UNABLE TO LOCATE / PENDING FOLLOW-UP]. At the time I cleared, conditions were [FACTUAL STATUS]. Follow-up required: [ACTION / NONE].
```

## Quality check

Before submission, confirm that:

- the date, times, exact location, and IDs agree throughout;
- reported statements are attributed to the person who made them;
- personal observations use clear, objective language;
- actions and notifications appear in chronological order;
- names, plate numbers, and contact details were verified when possible;
- conclusions, diagnoses, and assumptions were removed;
- all referenced photos, video, forms, and agency numbers are accounted for; and
- corrections are made transparently without deleting or concealing the original record.
