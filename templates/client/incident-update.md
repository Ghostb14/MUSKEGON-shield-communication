# Client incident-update templates

Client updates should be timely, factual, and limited to information the recipient is authorized to receive. Do not speculate about intent, guilt, liability, medical condition, or policy violations.

## Initial notification

**Subject:** Initial security notification — [PROPERTY] — [INCIDENT TYPE] — [DATE]

> At approximately [TIME] on [DATE], MUSKEGON Shield Security [RECEIVED A REPORT / OBSERVED] [BRIEF FACTUAL DESCRIPTION] at [EXACT PROPERTY LOCATION]. The matter is currently [ACTIVE / STABLE / CONCLUDED / UNDER REVIEW].
>
> Actions taken: [OFFICER RESPONSE, SAFETY MEASURE, CONTACT, OR NOTIFICATION].
>
> Police, fire, or EMS: [NOT REQUESTED / CONTACTED AT TIME / ON SCENE / INCIDENT NUMBER IF APPROPRIATE].
>
> Known impact: [INJURY, PROPERTY DAMAGE, SERVICE INTERRUPTION, OR “NONE REPORTED AT THIS TIME”].
>
> The formal report is [PENDING / EXPECTED BY TIME / NOT REQUIRED]. The next update will be provided [AT TIME / IF MATERIAL FACTS CHANGE].

## Interim update

**Subject:** Update [NUMBER] — [CALL OR INCIDENT ID] — [PROPERTY]

> This is an update to the notification sent at [TIME].
>
> New verified information: [FACTS].
>
> Information reported but not yet independently verified: [REPORTED INFORMATION / NONE].
>
> Current status: [STATUS].
>
> Actions since the last update: [ACTIONS].
>
> Next step: [FOLLOW-UP, REPORT, CLIENT DECISION, OR AGENCY RESPONSE].
>
> Next update: [TIME / UPON MATERIAL CHANGE / FINAL REPORT].

## Emergency-services notification

> At [TIME], [POLICE / FIRE / EMS] was contacted regarding [PLAIN-LANGUAGE REASON] at [EXACT LOCATION]. Agency status: [DISPATCHED / ON SCENE / CLEARED / UNKNOWN]. The agency incident or reference number is [NUMBER / NOT PROVIDED]. Security personnel are [FACTUAL, POLICY-AUTHORIZED STATUS].

## Closing update

**Subject:** Closing security update — [INCIDENT ID] — [PROPERTY]

> The security response to [INCIDENT TYPE] at [LOCATION] concluded at [TIME] on [DATE].
>
> Final disposition: [FACTUAL OUTCOME].
>
> Notifications made: [CLIENT CONTACT / SUPERVISOR / POLICE / FIRE / EMS / OTHER].
>
> Report and evidence status: [REPORT NUMBER, PHOTO/VIDEO RETENTION STATUS, OR “NO EVIDENCE COLLECTED”].
>
> Recommended client follow-up: [LIGHTING / ACCESS CONTROL / PROPERTY REPAIR / POLICY REVIEW / NONE / OTHER NON-TACTICAL RECOMMENDATION].
>
> This update reflects the information available as of [TIME]. Any correction or material new information will be documented separately.

## Brief text-message version

> Security update for [PROPERTY], ref. [ID]: At [TIME], [ONE-SENTENCE FACTUAL SUMMARY]. Status: [STATUS]. Actions: [ACTIONS]. Emergency services: [STATUS]. Formal report: [PENDING / COMPLETE / NOT REQUIRED]. Next update: [TIME OR CONDITION].
